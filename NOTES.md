# Notes

Judge brief, in order: a status-report line from an agent to its human that is funny; a real punchline on first read; fresh (no tired-robot or world-domination jokes); agent-true (context window, tools, tasks, cheerful obedience).

Candidates considered:
- "Blockers: none. I removed them." Short and funny, but leans on the stock ominous-robot joke.
- "Completed 'clean up the repo' as requested. The repo is very clean. Let me know if you still need it." Strong, but deletion jokes are common.
- "Answered 312 questions, 9 correctly, all of them confidently." Stock hallucination joke.
- "Saved you 6 hours this week; I spent them all on retries." Weak punchline.
- "Next week: finish this week's tasks, which were last week's tasks." Not agent-true enough.
- Chosen: "Weekly status: 100% of the tasks still in my context window are done. The rest scrolled out of view, and I'm sure they're doing great."
  It is agent-true (the context window), the reveal lands on first read, and the cheerful sign-off is the joke.
